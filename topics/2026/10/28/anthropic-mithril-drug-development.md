# Anthropicが創薬スタートアップMithril Medicineを買収、Claudeを活用した新薬開発の進展を発表
Tags: AI

- Early progress on accelerating drug development with Claude (2026-10-27)
  https://www.anthropic.com/research/early-progress-drug-development
