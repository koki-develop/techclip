# CISAがLangflow・Apache Tomcat・N-able N-centralの実悪用中の脆弱性をKEVカタログに追加
Tags: Security

- CISA Flags Langflow RCE, Tomcat, and N-central Flaws as Actively Exploited (2026-08-05)
  https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/cisa-flags-langflow-rce-tomcat-and-n.html
- CISA warns of hackers exploiting Langflow, N-central, Apache Tomcat flaws (2026-08-05)
  https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/cisa-warns-of-hackers-exploiting-langflow-n-central-apache-tomcat-flaws/
- CISA Warns of Exploited Langflow, N-central, and Tomcat Vulnerabilities (2026-08-04)
  https://www.securityweek.com/cisa-warns-of-exploited-langflow-n-central-and-tomcat-vulnerabilities/

CISAは実際に悪用が確認されているLangflowのリモートコード実行脆弱性(CVSS 9.8)、Apache Tomcatの暗号化バイパス、N-able N-centralの認証バイパスの3件を既知の悪用脆弱性(KEV)カタログに追加し、連邦機関に8月7日までの対応を義務付けた。
